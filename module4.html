Module 4: Guess the Sensor

Predict, then check: read a noisy response, estimate the hidden parameters, and submit your guess before revealing the sensor.

Hidden sensor

Use the time and amplitude coordinates to estimate the parameters. Tap or hover over the curve to read points. The response includes small measurement noise.

Your estimate

Each parameter earns 50 points when its relative error is at most 10%; otherwise it earns 0. Both within 10% earns 100/100. One scored submission per sensor.
