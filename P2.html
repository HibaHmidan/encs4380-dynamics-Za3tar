P2: A slow sensor in the loop

The controller never sees the true output y. It only sees ym, what the sensor reports. The sensor is the first-order system from Module 1: τs·dym/dt + ym = y. The error is e = r − ym.

Plant: K/(τs + 1) with K = 1, τ = 1 s. Setpoint r = 1. Control signal limited to −10 ≤ u ≤ 10. Simulated with our own solver: RK4, step h = 1 ms.
