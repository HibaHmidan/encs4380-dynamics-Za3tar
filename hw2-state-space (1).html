How the gains are calculated

Let x be displacement and v its rate of change. The plant obeys m·x″ + b·x′ + k·x = u.

Use state feedback u = −Kx·x − Kv·v. The resulting characteristic polynomial is s² + (b + Kv)/m · s + (k + Kx)/m.

For two real poles −a₁ and −a₂, the desired polynomial is (s + a₁)(s + a₂). Matching the two coefficients gives:

Kx = m·a₁·a₂ − k Kv = m·(a₁ + a₂) − b

For a damping ratio and natural frequency, the desired polynomial is s² + 2ζωn·s + ωn², so:

Kx = m·ωn² − k Kv = 2·m·ζ·ωn − b

A negative gain means the controller removes part of the plant's own spring or damping. The curves use RK4 with a 0.01 s step, with ideal state measurements. The force is unlimited unless you switch the limit on, which makes the loop nonlinear.
