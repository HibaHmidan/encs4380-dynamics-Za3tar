P1: PID playground

A controller compares the output y with the setpoint r and sends a control signal u to the plant: u = Kp·e + Ki·∫e dt + Kd·de/dt, with e = r − y. Change the gains and watch what each term does.

The loop

The loop is simulated with our own solver: RK4 for the plant, step h = 1 ms, PID updated every step. No closed-form answer is used.
