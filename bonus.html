Bonus: Our Euler ODE Solver

Compare our explicit Euler solver with the closed-form step response of the same mass–spring–damper system.

Model and numerical method

m x'' + b x' + k x = F(t). Here m = 1 kg, k = 4 N/m, and F(t) = 1 N for t ≥ 0. Initial position and velocity are zero. Final position = F/k = 0.25 m.

With v = x', the explicit Euler updates are:

x[n+1] = x[n] + h v[n]
v[n+1] = v[n] + h (1 − b v[n] − 4 x[n])

Both right-hand sides use the old values x[n] and v[n]. h is the time step in seconds. The solver is implemented below in JavaScript; Chart.js only draws the curves.

Experiment
